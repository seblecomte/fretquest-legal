# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Aurélia Pandora Labs** ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the **FretScape** mobile application ("the App"), formerly published under the working name FretQuest.

FretScape is **private by design**: the App does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send any data about you to our servers or to any third party.

## 1. Information we do not collect

FretScape does not collect, transmit or share:

- your name, email address, phone number or any other contact information;
- your location, contacts, calendar or photos;
- device or advertising identifiers (IDFA);
- analytics, crash logs or any usage data sent to remote servers;
- audio recordings or camera images.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks or authentication providers.

## 2. Microphone and camera

- **Microphone.** When you use the tuner or play an exercise on your real instrument, the App listens to the microphone to detect the pitch of the note you play. The audio is analysed **in real time, on your device only**. It is never recorded, stored or transmitted.
- **Camera.** Some versions of the App may offer an optional "Mirror" exercise mode that uses the camera to locate your fretting hand on your instrument's neck. Images are analysed **in real time, on your device only**. They are never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Both permissions are requested only when you choose a feature that needs them, and you can revoke them at any time in iOS Settings. The rest of the App works without them.

## 3. Information stored locally on your device

To work, the App stores the following on your device only (via the operating system's local storage):

- the lessons you have started and completed, and your training history;
- your personal records (e.g. timed mode);
- your preferences (instrument, notation, fretboard look, light/dark theme, input mode).

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the App erases it.

## 4. Children

FretScape is rated 4+ on the App Store. Because the App collects no personal information and shows no advertising, it is suitable for users of all ages and consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## 5. Future changes

If a future version introduces optional purchases, they will go through Apple's standard In-App Purchase system — Apple is the merchant of record and we never see your payment details. If we ever start collecting any data, this policy and the App Store "App Privacy" section will be updated **before** that version ships, and the App will tell you.

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information about you, there is no profile to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You keep full control over the data stored on your device.
