# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Aurélia Pandora Labs** ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the **FretQuest** mobile application ("the App").

We have designed FretQuest to be **privacy-friendly by default**: the App does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not transmit data about you to our servers or to any third party.

---

## 1. Information we do not collect

The current version of FretQuest **does not collect, transmit or share** any of the following:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
- Your location
- Your contacts, calendar, photos, or microphone input
- Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising IDs)
- Crash logs or analytics
- Any usage data sent to remote servers

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, third-party advertising networks, or third-party authentication providers.

---

## 2. Information stored locally on your device

To make the App functional, the following data is stored **on your device only**, using the operating system's local storage (Apple's `NSUserDefaults` / React Native's AsyncStorage):

- Your accumulated **XP** and **daily streak**
- The list of **lessons you have completed**
- Your **personal best times** for chrono mode
- Your **training history** (timestamps of completed sessions)
- Your **preferences** (note notation FR/EN, app language)

This data **never leaves your device**. If you delete the App, this data is erased with it. You can also clear it at any time from the App's Settings screen.

---

## 3. Children

FretQuest is rated **4+** in the App Store. It is suitable for children. Because the App collects no personal information and serves no advertisements, it is compliant with Apple's privacy requirements for apps aimed at children of all ages and with the U.S. **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**.

---

## 4. Third-party services

The current version of the App does **not** integrate any third-party SDK that processes user data, beyond the standard frameworks provided by Apple and the React Native runtime (which do not collect personal information about end-users).

In the future, we may introduce:

- **Advertising** (e.g. AdMob) — this Privacy Policy will be updated **before** any such integration ships, and the App Store listing's "App Privacy" section will be updated accordingly.
- **Optional subscriptions** through Apple's standard In-App Purchase system — Apple acts as the merchant of record. Apple's privacy policies apply to the purchase transaction. We never see your payment information.

---

## 5. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal information about you, there is no profile to access, modify, export or delete on our side. You retain full control of the locally-stored data described in Section 2 through your device.

---

## 6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are material (for example, if we start collecting personal data), we will display a notice inside the App.
